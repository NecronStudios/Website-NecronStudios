VOID / CURRENT CANON

THE ISLAND
REMEMBERS.

A complete public presentation of the current VOID history: from the Hope Archipelago and the first observation of the Dark Atom to the Dark Incident, the return of the organization and the Experiment VOID.

01 / BEFORE THE VOID

The Hope Archipelago

Before the Island of the Void existed, the Hope Archipelago surrounded a gigantic tropical main island: the Island of Hope. It was larger than the Amazon, rich in vegetation and biodiversity, and opened to tourism only once per year.

Paradise / restricted tourism / private transport infrastructure
02 / THE INSTALLATION

The Scientific Complex

Long before the Dark Incident, an organization already operated a large scientific installation on the island. The complex included advanced laboratories and a particle accelerator.

Research site / particle accelerator / classified organization
03 / FIRST OBSERVATION

Dark Atom

During an accelerator experiment, thousands of bright points pass through the equipment. One is completely black, moves more slowly than the others, keeps moving after shutdown, contorts and then disappears. The researchers give it the provisional name Dark Atom.

Unknown phenomenon / provisional term
04 / THE DOCUMENT

More Than 100 Pages

One scientist records observations, data, hypotheses, speculation, possible explanations and descriptions of the event. Another creates a sketch of the Dark Atom. The document reaches a superior and remains restricted.

Observation record / sketch / internal circulation
05 / FIRST LEAK

The First Leak

A hacker group penetrates the organization and releases the Dark Atom document online. Most of the public dismisses it as a hoax, creepypasta or conspiracy. The organization reinforces its digital security.

Public exposure / denial / security escalation
06 / YEARS OF FAILURE

Replication Fails

For years, the organization changes experimental conditions and studies its records, trying to reproduce the phenomenon. The original Dark Atom is never recreated directly.

Repeated experiments / no direct reproduction
07 / HARVARD

A New Attempt

Researchers from Harvard are invited to study the available data. They discuss possible methods and are taken to the accelerator for another attempt to reproduce the phenomenon.

External researchers / accelerator trial
08 / DARK INCIDENT

The Dark Incident

The accelerator emits a deep, rhythmic sound. A black shadow forms, particles move toward it, and the mass compresses until a black sphere appears. The surrounding space distorts. The object behaves like a black hole, but its gravitational effects do not match its size.

Internal termBlack Anomaly
Observed objectSmall black hole
IncidentDark Incident
09 / THREE MONTHS

The Island Begins to Die

The black hole remains active for approximately three months. The destruction is gradual: vegetation disappears, animals are affected, the sky closes in and the tropical environment becomes a dead landscape.

Solid matter is consumed / liquid water is not consumed
10 / THE ARCHIPELAGO

The Hope Archipelago Disappears

The smaller islands are gradually consumed. The gigantic Island of Hope survives, but loses an immense amount of territory and ends slightly smaller than the Amazon. It becomes the only major survivor of the old archipelago.

Island survives / archipelago does not
11 / THE AIRCRAFT

A Missing Field of Wreckage

On the day the Dark Incident begins, an aircraft crashes near the coast. Search teams find no conventional field of debris because the solid aircraft structure and wreckage are consumed, while the water remains.

Crash confirmed / solid wreckage consumed
12 / DISTANT EFFECTS

The Vanished Village

Thousands of kilometers away, a village disappears and leaves only a shallow depression. Other distant effects are recorded, including isolated animals in Madagascar found dead or affected in ways resembling radiation exposure. No global radioactive catastrophe occurs, and survivors exist.

Remote anomaly / survivors remain
13 / LOCKDOWN

The Island Is Closed

Travel is removed from tourism sites. Ports stop admitting people. Flights are canceled and refunded. The public sees the shutdown, but not the real cause, and the vanished aircraft fuels speculation.

Interdiction / public uncertainty
14 / END OF DARK INCIDENT

The Black Hole Disappears

After roughly three months, the black hole simply disappears. There is no final explosion or satisfactory explanation. The paradise is gone, the archipelago is gone and the island receives a new name:

THE ISLAND OF THE VOID
15 / SECOND LEAK

The World Finds Out

The same hacker group releases information about the Dark Incident. Governments, intelligence services, the FBI, scientists, the UN and other organizations investigate. The event becomes a major scientific mystery, followed by new international rules and regulations around high-risk research.

Second exposure / international investigation
16 / YEARS LATER

The World Forgets

Public attention fades. The Dark Incident becomes an old mystery. Some people continue to warn that it should not be forgotten. Most conclude that the organization was stopped and the island was abandoned.

Public memory / false conclusion
17 / THE RETURN

The Organization Returns

The organization never truly abandoned the island. It returns and builds or reorganizes a relatively discreet surface installation inside the dead forest. Offices, conventional laboratories and administrative areas sit above a much larger underground complex.

Surface facility / underground facility
18 / THE UNDERGROUND COMPLEX

The Experiment VOID Wing

Beneath the surface are advanced laboratories, particle accelerators, experimental sectors, storage, maintenance, security and containment. One sector becomes central to the new work: the Experiment VOID wing.

Containment / experimental research / underground sectors
19 / TERMINOLOGY CHANGES

Dark Atom Becomes Dark Matter

With scientific progress, the organization understands the substance better. Dark Atom and dark matter are the same substance; the terminology changes with the level of knowledge. Older documents use the former name, later documents use the latter.

One substance / two eras of terminology
20 / ARTIFICIAL ACQUISITION

Obtaining Small Samples

The original Dark Atom phenomenon still cannot be reproduced directly. With more advanced technology, the organization develops another method involving the manipulation of antimatter, allowing it to obtain small quantities of dark matter.

Not a recreation of the original event / small samples
21 / MULTIPLICATION

Dark Matter Multiplies

When dark matter comes into contact with normal matter, it can multiply. Even a small sample therefore becomes dangerous, because the surrounding environment itself can provide material for continued growth.

Contact risk / containment becomes critical
22 / VACUUM CONTAINMENT

The Vacuum Reservoir

Samples are held inside a specialized vacuum reservoir designed to isolate the substance from normal matter. Preventing contact with the environment becomes one of the installation's most important containment problems.

Isolation / containment infrastructure
23 / EXPERIMENT VOID

What the Organization Wants

The Experiment VOID is designed to study how dark matter interacts with living organisms, how it can alter biological properties, how those properties might be controlled and how the discovery could be turned into something useful for humanity.

Biological interaction / control / utility
24 / HUMAN SUBJECTS

People Become Material

The organization begins using human beings as test subjects, including children, women and men. Some children are orphans; other victims are obtained through illegal networks involving human trafficking, migrant smuggling and exploitation.

Human experimentation / non-consensual subjects
25 / THE MOTIVE

Greed

The stated drive is not simply to create a weapon. The organization wants to transform a potentially revolutionary discovery into technology that can serve humanity. The reason it crosses ethical boundaries is greed.

Technology / ambition / ethical collapse
26 / THE DEEPER POSSIBILITY

Something May Be Watching

The Dark Incident may not be the first event of its kind to exist, only the first recorded by humanity. A possible entity or intelligence of cosmic scale may be connected to the chain of events. The possibility is never directly confirmed.

Clues / no definitive explanation
27 / HORROR COSMICO

The Object of Study

A human being may be an observation sample. A family may be a sample. A village, ecosystem, island or species may be a sample. Perhaps there is no hatred or revenge — only observation, experimentation and data collection.

Maybe humanity was never the target

THE QUESTION

What was
Dark Atom?

The current canon does not provide a definitive answer. The mystery remains open.

The unanswered question is part of the world, not a missing chapter.
